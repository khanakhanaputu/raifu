## Overall Impression

The landing page is well-crafted where it commits to the brand (Philosophy section, footer's "ketenangan dan ruang kosong" line) and generic where it defaults to SaaS-marketing habit (Features grid, star-rated testimonials). The single biggest opportunity: the Hero's scan card — the page's visual centerpiece and its one licensed shadow — currently contradicts the product's own "no guilt / no precision-obsession" thesis with fabricated AI-confidence chrome, before the philosophy that would justify calm restraint has even been introduced.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Hero scan card fabricates AI precision the product doesn't have**
- Why it matters: "94.8% Akurasi Scan AI" (line 19), "AI Vision 2.4" (line 184), and "Keyakinan: 98%" (line 203) present a scripted/mock scanner (confirmed in PRODUCT.md) with real-product-grade confidence chrome. This is the exact overclaim PRODUCT.md's "Honesty about demo scope" principle warns against, on the very first thing a skeptical visitor evaluates.
- Fix: strip the fabricated confidence number and version tag, or visibly reframe as an illustrative preview rather than a live scan result.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P1] Triple shadow use violates DESIGN.md's Flat-Except-One Rule**
- Why it matters: DESIGN.md reserves the one shadow for the hero card only, but the page has three: the hero card itself (line 178, correct), the "14 Hari Streak" badge (`shadow-sm`, line 173), and the scanline glow (`shadow-[0_0_12px_2px_...]`, line 208). A named, explicit design-system rule is broken on the flagship page.
- Fix: drop `shadow-sm` from the badge and the glow effect from the scanline; keep exactly one shadow on the page.
- Suggested command: /impeccable polish

**[P2] Hero card cognitive overload**
- Why it matters: the card bundles ~12 discrete data points (version tag, 4 corner labels, scanline glow, kcal total, tagline, 3 macro rows with bars, save button) in one viewport — 3x over the ≤4-item working-memory limit — directly contradicting the "Quiet Ryokan" premise before any justifying philosophy appears.
- Fix: cut overlay labels from 4 to 2, remove the redundant confidence stat, let the kcal total alone carry the "considered fact" weight per the Serif-For-Facts Rule.
- Suggested command: /impeccable distill

**[P2] Redundant nav destinations**
- Why it matters: "Masuk" and "Mulai Sekarang" (site-nav.tsx:72-83) both route to `/masuk`, and the header carries 8 total interactive targets — friction for a scanning visitor for zero benefit.
- Fix: differentiate the two ("Masuk" → login-focused state, "Mulai Sekarang" → signup-focused state) or drop one.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P3] Unexplained jargon at first exposure**
- Why it matters: "Hara Hachi Bu: 80% Kenyang" appears in the Hero (line 215) roughly 230 lines before it's explained in Philosophy (350-357) — zero context on first read.
- Fix: add a 3-4 word inline gloss, or scroll-link the hero tag to `#filosofi`.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify
